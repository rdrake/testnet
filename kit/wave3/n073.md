With `echo-message` and `message-tags`, a client that sent `@+example/tag=x NOTICE nick :text` got its echo without `+example/tag`. The recipient, the history row and the bouncer session copies all carried the tag. Only the sender's own echo in `relay_private_notice()` was built without it.

**The fix:** `relay_private_notice()` now echoes the way `relay_private_message()` has since 7f53d940.
- With client tags, the echo goes through `sendcmdto_one_client_tags()`, which adds the line's label or batch and the event's msgid and time, on the sender's own caps.
- Without client tags, the echo is unchanged.
- The `#else` copy, which configure never builds, is removed. Storage stays under `USE_ROCKSDB`.

**Testing:** build and all CMocka suites pass. On a running server:
- The echo of a tagged NOTICE now carries `+example/tag=x` and the same msgid as the recipient's copy.
- A plain NOTICE's echo is unchanged, and so is a tagged PRIVMSG's.
