When the async log ring was full, `log_async_write()` waited once (1 ms) for `not_full`. If that wait returned 0, it wrote at `head` without checking again. A spurious wakeup, or another producer that took the freed slot first, then overwrote the oldest unwritten entry and moved `head` onto `tail`. The ring then looked empty, and the writer thread wrote none of what was queued.

**The fix:** wait in a loop until there is room or the 1 ms deadline passes. If the ring is still full at the deadline, take the existing direct-write fallback.

**Testing:** `log_async_cmocka` gains a case that wraps `pthread_cond_timedwait()` to deliver one spurious wakeup on a full ring. It fails before the change and passes after; all suites pass.
