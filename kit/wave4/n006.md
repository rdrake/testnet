The stored bouncer session record takes the primary's nick when the record is written, and nothing marked the session dirty on a rename. After the primary's own NICK, an SVSNICK or a device's request, the record kept the old nick until something else dirtied the session. A crash-restore then brought the session back under the old nick.

**The fix:** `set_nick_name()` marks the session whose primary (or held ghost) was renamed, and the next persist tick writes the record.

**Testing:** build and all CMocka suites pass. There is no unit test for the path through `set_nick_name()`.
