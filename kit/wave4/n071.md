`paste_listener_shutdown()` closes every open paste connection (TLS close_notify, `socket_del()`, the listener reference), but nothing called it. On DIE or RESTART those descriptors were left to `close_connections()`, which closes by number.

**The fix:** `shutdown_stores()` calls it right after the listeners stop. The unused variable in `paste_parse_filename_hint()` is removed as well.

**Testing:** build and all CMocka suites pass. A server with a paste listener, stopped with SIGTERM, now logs `Paste listener: shutdown`.
