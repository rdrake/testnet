2. **Nothing enforces "a worker touches nothing of the main thread".** By reading. The rules are comments, and the log mute turns any future `log_write`/`Debug` on a worker into a silent drop. A later edit that adds `feature_int()`, `FindChannel()`, `CurrentTime`, a `send*` or a static-buffer helper to `history_query_internal`, `search_row_hook` or `presence_row_hook` compiles, passes every test and races: `chathistory_search_job_cmocka` runs the job on one thread with a fake pool, so the real hooks never run on a worker. 3. **A full wait list stalls again, and late jobs are scanned for nobody.** Plausible, by reading. Past `STORE_JOBS_WAIT_MAX` (64, global) a job runs inline on the main loop (`ircd/store_job.c:277`). With the default pool (3 scans at once) a full list drains in about 64/3 × 0.25 s = 5.3 s, past the default `CHATHISTORY_TIMEOUT` of 5, so the tail is answered after the requester gave up. One peer can fill it and delay every other peer's scans; with #252 as it stands one client can queue about 40 parts per peer in one burst (see #252). Whatever the answer below: abandon a job older than `CHATHISTORY_TIMEOUT` before `run()`; optionally a per-link quota.
