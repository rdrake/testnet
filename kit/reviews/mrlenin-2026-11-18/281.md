# #281 recv: a client's overlong line gets ERR_INPUTTOOLONG, not a disconnect

Head when copied: f480f76c

## MrLenin, 2026-11-18T19:10:06Z

Reviewed at f480f76c. Debug build, CMocka passes; base and head were run on a scratch server over plain TCP, TLS and WebSocket; irctest not run.

Run before and after registration: a 600-byte body, a 5000-byte tag region, tags plus an overlong body, a labeled line, a line inside an open multiline batch, a CR/LF split across reads, a 60000-byte line, a 70000-byte line with no terminator, and a burst of 40 lines of 10 KB in one write. The base disconnects in every case. The head answers 417 and the next `PING` and stays connected, except: the unterminated line quits with `Excess Flood: line too long`, and the burst gets two 417s, then `Excess Flood` from the recvQ cap.

No parser desync found. The injection case run: `PRIVMSG x :` plus 520 `A`s, then `QUIT :INJECTED-TAIL`, in three reads 300 ms apart, over plain TCP and TLS: 417, `PONG`, and the `QUIT` did not run. Not run over WebSocket (`ws_deliver()` checked by reading).

1. An `AUTHENTICATE` line over 512 bytes now gets 417 and never reaches `m_authenticate()`, so the exchange stays open. Run: `AUTHENTICATE PLAIN`, a 600-byte `AUTHENTICATE`, then good credentials: 417, then 900/903. The description implies a 905 that is not sent. Fix: in the overlong branch of `parse_client_line()` (`ircd/parse.c:1553`), when the cut line is `AUTHENTICATE` and an exchange is in flight, end it as the 905 path does (`ircd/m_authenticate.c:276-285`); or reword the claim.
2. By reading, plausible, not measured: bytes past the caps never reach the recvQ or count toward Excess Flood; a cut line costs only the flat `lagmin + BUFSIZE / lagfactor` (about 6 s by default, `ircd/parse.c:1570`). A ~64 KiB line every 6 s is never dropped: about 11 KB/s of discarded input, some 14 times what legal lines allow. Small and bounded; if wanted, charge the cut bytes (`lagmin + min(cut_bytes, N) / lagfactor`), or drop a client after a few consecutive overlong lines.

Deploy: the client quit reasons `Excess Flood: tag region too large` and `Excess Flood: message region too large` are gone; a line over 64 KiB quits with `Excess Flood: line too long`. WebSocket clients no longer get Close 1009 for an overlong IRC line (a frame over 16 KiB still does). Log alerts matching the old text need updating.
