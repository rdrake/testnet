The spec's "NEW can also update the value" has no form for removing one, so a client may keep the old `attach-cursor`; a DEL plus NEW would cancel the cap for everyone. Proposed: keep it and say so in `readme.features`; the flag is an operator switch.

**Mixed builds / deploy:** nothing changes on the S2S wire or on disk. With the flag FALSE, Seance develop (requests `draft/persistence` only) gets restored JOINs unwrapped, so its restoration handler does not run, autojoin is not held and they read as live activity; with no `attach-cursor` it sends no `PERSISTENCE ATTACH default <msgid>` and does its own catch-up. The extras work. A network serving Seance builds without evilnet/seance#327 needs `PERSISTENCE_DRAFT_EXTRAS = TRUE` until those ship; `readme.features` could say so.
