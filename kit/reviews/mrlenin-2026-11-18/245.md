Fixed since the last review: two same-nick sessions of one account after a roll-forward (run: one ghost, one burst `N`); newer-build records reported in one line per start (run); the eviction summary counts what it deletes. f2d45810 does what its description says, and no defect was found in it.

Q2 (expire) and Q3 (skip) are implemented as decided. Runs, with session X (account `carol`, nick `carol`, in `#x`) held by the newer build, then a rollback to this PR:
- Same account: `carol` logs in again as session Y (`#y`); roll forward. One ghost `carol` comes back, Y; X's record is deleted. carol's next login revives Y. Nobody is logged out; `#x` is lost, not merged.
- Different accounts: during the rollback account `dave` registers with nick `carol` (Y, `#y`); roll forward. Y comes back as `carol`; X is not restored this start and its record kept. carol's login asking for `carol` gets 433 and a new session with no channels; dave's login is revived, told `NICK carol`, and keeps `#y`.

Consequences an operator would not expect:

1. **`BOUNCER_MAX_SESSIONS` is now enforced at every restoring start, not only after a rollback** (by reading; not run). `bounce_restore_prepare()` applies the cap at every restore (`ircd/bouncer_session.c:5795-5797`) and deletes an over-cap session's record (`:5818-5825`; the cut is `ircd/bouncer_record.c:554-566`). An operator who lowers the cap and restarts loses the extra sessions, sessions active at a crash included; on the tip all were restored. Only a per-record warning says so; the description's Operators notes should state it.
2. **Without #260, a crash inside the persist window now drops a live session for that start** (run, `BOUNCER_PERSIST_INTERVAL` 30): A renames `foo` to `bar`, account B registers as `foo`, `kill -9`. On restart B's session comes back as `foo`; A's, active at the crash, is not restored this start, as its record still says `foo`. #260 removes the cause (the record is written at the rename), so #260 needs to go in no later than this PR.
3. **The skipped other-account record is not counted against the cap** (run): at the next clean restart X is skipped again and carol's new session restored, so carol's account holds two records with `BOUNCER_MAX_SESSIONS` 1 until X's hold ends. The description should say so.
4. Still open: no test runs the real startup restore (`bounce_restore_prepare()`, `bounce_db_restore()`) over mixed-version records, as the description says.

Could be simpler: the maintainers have asked to keep machinery for the rollback-then-roll-forward round trip to a minimum, and to stop over-engineering where it isn't necessary. Q3 alone is met by the `FindClient()` guard in the restore loop (`ircd/bouncer_session.c:6053-6063`, about 12 lines). The selection pass with its candidate collection and logging, the eviction pass's change for migrated records, and their CMocka cases are about 300 lines beyond that. Cutting them would lose: the latest-active choice (the winner of a same-nick pair would be decided by store key order, so a user may get back the pre-rollback session instead of the one used during the rollback); the deletion of the loser (it lingers, skipped at each start, until its hold ends); and the cap at restore (as on the tip, which also removes item 1). Each loss is at worst that someone reconnects and rejoins channels; keeping them buys a deterministic choice and a cap that holds at every restore.
