Could be simpler: commit 70b712b1 copies members' markers to the new name at a rename or relocation: `markread_channel_renamed()` (`m_markread.c:662`), called from `rename_channel()` (`channel.c:2416`), `relocate_execute()` (`m_rename.c:2634`) and `relocate_move_issuer()` (`m_rename.c:2813`). The copy is not relayed, so builds diverge (run: one fork server on the head and one on the tip; after a rename `MARKREAD #xnew` answered the copied marker on one and `timestamp=*` on the other for the same account, until its next newer set). Your earlier state, c8144d77, sends `*` for the new name until the user next reads, which is all the spec asks; what is lost is the renamed channel's read point until then. RENAME is low priority on our side for now, and it is refused while a stock server is linked, so on a network that still links stock servers the copy never runs.
