# #285 sasl: abort an exchange that waits for the client when it registers

Head when copied: 2f29456a

## MrLenin, 2026-11-18T19:10:11Z

Reviewed at 2f29456a. Built at the head, CMocka suites pass, and base and head were run on a scratch server against fake P10 services; the Keycloak and IAuth tiers are covered by CMocka only, irctest not run.

Every behavioural claim in the description held. `AUTHENTICATE PLAIN` then `CAP END` got nothing until the SASL timeout on the base, and `AUTHENTICATE +`, 906 and 001 at once on the head, with services sent `D A`. Pipelined credentials still log in, in either order around `CAP END`. A late services answer for the aborted exchange is dropped, and a new exchange after it logs in under its own cookie. Mutation check: each of four mutations of the head (no abort in `check_auth_finished()`, the turn handed back on every challenge, no `SASLX_COMPLETING`, the registration abort counted) made the matching new tests fail. The mechanism-name check also stops a line that is not a mechanism name from being relayed to services as one (seen on the base in a run).

1. Low, by reading: a refused OAUTHBEARER token (local error challenge, `SASL_STATE_FAILED`) whose client then completes registration instead of sending `*` gets 904 and is not counted toward `SASL_MAX_FAILURES` (`authenticate_end()`, `ircd/m_authenticate.c:228`, `&& !for_registration`). The refusal is the client's, and on the tip it is counted at the `*`. Effect: the limit before registration is effectively 6 instead of 5. Fix: count the FAILED case even when `for_registration` (keep the 904); only aborts of data not yet judged should be free.
2. Low, run: with no mechanism list known (services sent no `M`, `SASL_DEFAULT_MECHANISMS` empty, no local tier), `AUTHENTICATE plain` gets a bare 904 without 908 (`ircd/m_authenticate.c:353-356`). Harmless, since X3 compares mechanism names case-sensitively. Optional: with no list, accept a name that is valid RFC 4422 once upper-cased, or send 908 with `SASL_DEFAULT_MECHANISMS`.

Deploy: a client that sends `CAP END` before its credentials, and whose backend's challenge arrives first, now gets 906 and registers without a login, where the tip held registration and logged it in (run). That is the spec's behaviour, but such a client loses its login, and with it any bouncer session it would have resumed. Correctly ordered pipelining is unaffected. On the wire only the existing `SASL ... D A` is used.
