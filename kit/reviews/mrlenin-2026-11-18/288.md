Not run: irctest, ASan.

Nothing blocking. What a pre-302 client gets (run, every capability feature on):
- `CAP LS` / `CAP LS 301` before registration: the tip sends two unmarked lines (501 bytes with 30 capabilities, then 109 bytes with 6); this head sends one line of the names without a `/`: 19 capabilities, `sasl` kept, 257 bytes.
- After registration with a 30-character nick: one line, 286 bytes (the tip: two).
- The narrowed names take 229 bytes, so the second cut is never needed: with a 75-character server name and a 30-character nick the line is 345 bytes, leaving 165.
- `CAP LS 302` is identical to the tip.

Optional follow-up:

1. **A pre-302 `CAP LIST` that does not fit silently drops capabilities the client holds.** Run: a pre-302 client holding 33 capabilities gets one line of 28; `server-time`, `setname`, `soju.im/search`, `standard-replies` and `userhost-in-names` are missing; the tip sent a second unmarked line. Where: the `break` in `send_caplist_lines()` (`ircd/m_cap.c:707-717`), logged only at Debug level. Unlikely: it needs a pre-302 client that blindly requested about 30 capabilities. Fix: for LIST keep the tip's unmarked continuation, or leave out the `/` names first as LS does and only then cut; either way log the cut at `L_INFO`.

Description: "With every feature on, the full list is over 500 bytes, so this is the usual case" holds only with some of the opt-in drafts on. With the defaults (opt-in drafts off, `sasl` on, no authtoken) the names take 384 bytes and fit one line with 98 bytes to spare (10 with the longest server name and nick).
