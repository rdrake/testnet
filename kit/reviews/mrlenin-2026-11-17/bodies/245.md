## Add at the end

```
**Changed after review:**
- **A roll-forward no longer restores two sessions under one nick.** While the older build ran, the user it could not restore reconnected and got a second session, usually under the same nick, and the roll-forward brought both back as ghosts under that nick (past `BOUNCER_MAX_SESSIONS`, and written again at every clean stop). The first pass now collects every session it would restore, and `bounce_restore_select()` decides which restore together:
  - Of sessions under one nick, the one active latest is kept. The other's record is deleted when it is the same account's. When it is another account's, it is only not restored this start; its record stays until its hold ends.
  - Then, per account, the `BOUNCER_MAX_SESSIONS` active latest are kept, and the rest deleted.
  - Each is logged, with one summary line. The restore loop also refuses to build a ghost whose nick is already held.
- The eviction pass applies the restore rule to every version it reads, so a migrated record kept that way is not evicted with the unreadable ones. Its summary counts what it deleted and the unreadable records.
- Newer-build records are reported in one line per start, not one per record.
- `bouncer_record_cmocka` covers `bounce_restore_select()`, including the review's case. `bounce_restore_prepare()` and `bounce_db_restore()` themselves still have no unit test.

**Operators:** turning the bouncer off to break a crash loop now drops the sessions the breaker names at that start; before, they were dropped at the next start with the bouncer on. A lone bad-size or unknown-version record is now deleted at the next start. New log lines include the L_CRIT "deleting session ... (a v%u record, of a newer build)".

**Open:**
- A session live when a newer build crashed, after which an older build ran: expire it at the roll-forward (as now), or keep holding it from the crash (which needs the older build to keep the newer one's last heartbeat).
- A session whose nick another account's restored session holds: skip it (as now), or restore it under a fallback nick.
```
