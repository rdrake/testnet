## Add at the end

```
**Changed after review:**
- The bullet about bouncer devices is gone: `bounce_recompute_session_caps()` copies each connection's own caps into its active set, so `CapActive` already equals `CapOwnHas`. The `CapOwnHas` edits stay because they read better, but they change no behaviour.
- **cap-notify is sticky for a CAP 302 client**, as the capability-negotiation spec says. `CAP REQ :-cap-notify` from such a client is refused (NAK), an ACK of `-cap-notify` is ignored, and `CAP CLEAR` leaves it. Below 302 it stays an ordinary capability. `cap_version_cmocka` covers the rule.
- **A change of a capability's value alone** (`MULTILINE_MAX_LINES`, `CHATHISTORY_MAX`, the chathistory retention) reaches CAP 302 clients with the new value and older clients not at all. Before, an older client got a bare CAP NEW for a capability it already had. Turning a capability on is still a NEW for everyone.
```
