## Add at the end

```
**Changed after review:** while `PERSISTENCE_DRAFT_EXTRAS` is TRUE, a client written against the old names sees no change:
- a connection without the vendor capability gets the restore batch typed `draft/persistence`;
- `draft/persistence` carries the extras' value too, where such clients read `attach-cursor` (set at start and when the flag changes);
- every connection reaches the extras, with or without a capability, as before (a script, or an oper's `/quote PERSISTENCE LIST`).

With the flag FALSE, the extras need the vendor capability, `draft/persistence` has no value, and every restore batch has the vendor type. `readme.features` says this, and that the account metadata keys (`draft/persistence/hold`, `.../auto-replay`, `.../profile/*`) keep their names, since renaming them would migrate the stores and the S2S wire.

**Mixed builds:** nothing changes on the S2S wire or on disk. On rollback, a config that sets `CAP_evilnet_persistence` or `PERSISTENCE_DRAFT_EXTRAS` gets a harmless "Unknown feature" CONFIG error.

**Open:**
- Whether the extras stay `PERSISTENCE` subcommands under the vendor capability, or move to a vendor command of their own.
- Whether cap-less use of the extras ends with this change or when `PERSISTENCE_DRAFT_EXTRAS` goes FALSE (this PR now does the second).
- The order with evilnet/seance#327 for turning the flag off.
- The capability's name form: `evilnet.github.io/persistence`, or `evilnet/<name>` as `evilnet/channel-relocate` uses.
```
