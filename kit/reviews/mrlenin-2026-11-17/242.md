Reviewed at acefc7e1. Built (debug, no warnings, 96 CMocka suites pass), and the cases below were run on two linked servers. The suffix refusals, the `from=` unescape and the MARKREAD refusals behave as described.

1. In a mixed network, a responder with this PR walks from the wrong place when a requester on the current tip sends a non-`Z` timestamp. Run, client on the tip server, rows only on the PR server: `BEFORE timestamp=<time of m6>+00:00` returned m1..m6 and a later JOIN; `AFTER timestamp=<time of m1>+00:00` (or with a lowercase `z`) returned only a JOIN; both with `draft/chathistory-end`. Tip to tip, both are correct. The tip requester sends the timestamp bare on `CH Q`; your `history_iso_to_unix()` refuses it, and the store walks fall back to "assume it's already Unix format" (`ircd/history.c:2371`, `:2412`, `:2457`, same pattern at `:2488`, `:2710`, `:2963`, `:2977`), keying the walk from the ISO text. Fix: in `ms_chathistory()` after the reference parse (`ircd/m_chathistory.c:8620`), answer `E <reqid> 0 U` when a TIMESTAMP reference containing `-` does not convert. Better, let the history.c fallback apply only to unix-shaped values (no `-`), which also covers the other callers.
2. No test was added. `history_iso_to_unix()` can be tested alone: accept `Z`, end of string, more than three fractional digits, no fraction; refuse `+02:00`, `+00:00`, `z`, `Zjunk`.

Description: the SEARCH example (191-byte channel, 250-byte `text=`) was not cut on the tip; its line measured 498 bytes. It is refused because `SEARCH_FED_FIXED` counts every field at its maximum. A real cut needs a text that escapes long (`q`, 170 spaces, `NOPE` in a 192-byte channel: cut at 510, and the peer returned a row without the text); that is refused now too. The check also runs on a single server whenever `in=` is a channel.

Not decided on our side yet: whether a timestamp with an explicit zero offset (`+00:00`, what Python's `datetime.isoformat()` gives for an aware UTC time) should be accepted.
- Accept it: such clients keep working, as on the tip.
- Refuse it, as the PR does: `Z` is the only UTC spelling, and such clients get `FAIL ... INVALID_PARAMS`.
