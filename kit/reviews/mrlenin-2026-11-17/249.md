A client that sent `CAP LS 302` can still disable cap-notify. `CAP REQ :-cap-notify` gets `ACK :-cap-notify`, after which `CAP LIST` no longer shows it although NEW/DEL keep arriving; `CAP CLEAR` acks `-cap-notify` too. The spec: "servers MUST NOT disable the `cap-notify` capability if the client requests `CAP LS` with a version of 302 or newer." Run, the same on the tip. Expected: a NAK of the whole request, as `cap_req()` does for a sticky capability, and `cap_clear()` skipping it. In `ircd/m_cap.c`, add `|| (neg && cap->cap == CAP_CAPNOTIFY && cli_capab_version(sptr) >= 302)` to the NAK condition in `cap_req()` (:782-784), and the same skip in `cap_clear()` (:883), optionally `cap_ack()` (:845). A pre-302 client may still drop it, and does correctly today.

Skip that NEW for a non-302 client; the callers in `ircd_features.c` know the case and can pass a flag to `send_cap_notify()` or store it in `cap_batch_entry`.
