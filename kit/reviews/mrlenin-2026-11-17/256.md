On a TOPIC that arrives untagged (from a stock link), `do_settopic()` mints the topic's msgid (`ircd/m_topic.c:176-186`), then reveals the hidden setter (:188-196), which mints the JOIN's id and writes its row, and only then reads `topic_time_ms` (:203). The TOPIC's live `@time`, row and `@A` time drift from its msgid's mint time by the reveal's work; the tip read the time right after the mint. By reading. Move the reveal block above the msgid block: that restores the order, and the JOIN's id sorts before the TOPIC it precedes.
