A `CHATHISTORY` page for a private conversation, asked for with the nick in another case (`CHATHISTORY LATEST FOO * 10` for `foo`), sent the requester's own rows to `FOO`. `replay_name_after_request()` copied the request into the rows' target as typed.

Nicks compare without case, but the rows were delivered to `foo`. A client that keys buffers by target, or compares replayed lines with the ones it saw live, got a second spelling of the same conversation.

### What changes

- **The batch** keeps the request's spelling, as before.
- **The requester's own rows** go to the nick as the client holding it spells it now (`FindUser()`), and as typed when nobody holds it.
- **SEARCH.** `SEARCH in:<nick>` does the same.

The whole change is 17 lines in `replay.c` and `m_chathistory.c`. The lookup is by the requested nick, so only the letter case can change, never the nick.

### Which spelling

The rule is "as the nick's holder spells it now", not "as the rows were delivered". After a partner changes `Foo` to `fOo`, the requester's own rows replay as `PRIVMSG fOo`, while the partner's rows keep the source `Foo` they were sent under. Three cases get a less exact spelling than a derivation from the rows would give, all in letter case only:
- the partner is offline, or has renamed away and the nick is free: the request's case;
- the nick is now held by another account: that holder's case.

### Not changed

`CHATHISTORY TARGETS` and the bouncer's automatic replay name partners as before this PR.

### Tests

- **The whole CMocka suite passes** (debug build, no warnings). There is no unit test for the lookup itself.
- **irctest** `testChathistoryDMs` passed with the earlier, larger version of this change; it was not re-run after the cut.

**Changed after review:** the earlier version derived the spelling from the rows (`replay_name.h`, `replay_pm_request_name()`, a change to `pm_other_nick_from_messages()` that also touched TARGETS and the bouncer replay, and a CMocka suite: 234 lines). It is replaced by the lookup above, as the review suggested, since private-message history is to move onto the channel-history mechanism and new PM-only code should stay small.
