[account-tag](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/account-tag): "the account tag MUST be added to all messages sent by the user". The INVITE a local invitee receives went out through `sendcmdto_one()`, which tags nothing outside a labeled batch, so it had no `@account` and no `@time`. The invite-notify copies on different servers also carried different msgids.

### What changes

- Every delivery to a local invitee now uses `sendcmdto_one_event()`, as other per-recipient events do: `m_invite`, `ms_invite` for an inviter on another server, and an invite to a channel that does not exist. The line gets `@time`, `@account` and the other event tags the recipient's caps allow, and never the invitee's own label or open batch.
- The invite has one msgid and one time on every server. The origin mints them (`ms_invite` takes the origin's from the S2S tags) before any copy goes out, and every server-bound INVITE carries them: the direct one to a remote invitee, and the channel fanout, through `sendcmdto_channel_servers_butone()`, which now builds a tagged line for IRCv3-aware links when tags are wanted, as `sendcmdto_serv_butone()` does. The invitee's copy and every invite-notify copy, on any server, carry the same msgid and `@time`.

### Not in this PR

- A bouncer alias connection still doesn't get the INVITE, before or after this change. That needs alias fan-out like the one PRIVMSG has.
- The multiline `MULTILINE_INVALID` change that was here is now #298.

### Mixed builds

Toward an older fork server, that server mints its own msgid for its invitee and observers; from one, the bare INVITE arrives and this build mints its own. A stock peer gets the bare INVITE, as before. Nothing is stored, so a rollback is clean.

### Tests

- **irctest** (Nefarious controller): `account_tag.py` `testInvite` now passes. The full runs with and without X3 show no new failures.
- **CMocka `send_s2s_tags_cmocka`:** the channel-servers send carries the armed msgid when tags are wanted, is bare otherwise or with tags off, and leaves nothing armed.
- **The whole CMocka suite passes.**
