A client line with more than 4095 bytes of tags, or a body over 512 bytes, used to disconnect the client ("Excess Flood: tag region too large" / "message region too large"). message-tags says tag data over the limit MUST get `417 ERR_INPUTTOOLONG`, and other servers answer an overlong body the same way. An `AUTHENTICATE` line over 512 bytes was also disconnected, so the client only ever saw the 906 sent as it exited, never `m_authenticate()`'s 905. It now gets that 905 in place of the 417, and its SASL exchange ends (see "Changed after review").

### What changes

- **Cutting the line.** Client bytes now go through `recv_clip()` in recv_classify.c. It runs the same per-region state machine as `recv_classify()`, kept on the Connection across reads. It keeps each region's bytes up to one past its cap and drops the rest in place, so the recvQ never holds more than a capped line.
- **Answering it.** `parse_client()` checks `recv_line_overlong()` before it reads the line's tags or command, and answers 417. The client stays connected.
  - When only the body is over its cap, the tag region is whole and only its `label=` is read, so the 417 answers the line's label under the usual labeled-response check. A line cut in its tags gets an unlabeled 417, because its label may be the part that was cut.
  - The line is charged fake lag like a line with a full body (`lagmin + BUFSIZE / lagfactor`), so a client can't get 417s answered at wire speed.
  - If a multiline batch is open, it fails with `FAIL BATCH MULTILINE_INVALID` (no context, as the multiline spec defines it) and its reference stays dead. The FAIL goes out under the batch's own label, never the cut line's. The cut line may have been part of the batch, and delivering the rest would change the message.
- **What still disconnects.** A single line over 64 KiB still drops the client ("Excess Flood: line too long"). Servers and handshakes keep `recv_classify()` and its disconnect.
- **Line state follows the recvQ.** STARTTLS now resets the classifier's line state when it drops the recvQ, and the bouncer transplant moves that state along with the recvQ. A line split across either is no longer cut against a stale count.
- **Unchanged:** the caps themselves. The body cap is still 512 bytes, not counting CR LF.

### Tests

- **CMocka, `recv_classify_cmocka`:** covers `recv_clip()`, `recv_line_overlong()` and `recv_classify_reset()`.
  - A read split inside the tags or inside the body is cut the same as one whole read.
  - A line exactly at the cap is left alone.
  - The fatal bound is exact.
- **CMocka, `parse_line_cmocka`:**
  - An overlong line gets 417 and reaches no handler.
  - A 4095-byte tag region and a 512-byte body still run.
  - The fake-lag charge is exact.
  - An open multiline batch fails.
  - A line cut only in its body gets a labeled 417; a line cut in its tags, or with labeled-response not in use, an unlabeled one.
- **CMocka, `ml_discard_cmocka`:** the batch's FAIL answers the batch's label and leaves the cut line's label as it was.
- **The whole CMocka suite passes.**
- **irctest** (Nefarious controller, with and without X3 services): `testLineTooLong`, `testLengthLimits` and `testSaslTooLong` now pass, and nothing that passed before fails.

**Changed after a second review:** When an overlong line carries several `label=` tags, its 417 now goes under the last one, as on a line of legal length. Before, it went under the first.

**Changed after review:** a cut `AUTHENTICATE` line got 417 and never reached `m_authenticate()`, so the exchange stayed open and took the client's next `AUTHENTICATE <mechanism>` for its data. `parse_client()` now hands such a line to `authenticate_too_long()`, the 905 path `m_authenticate()` already had for a parameter over 400 bytes: 905 (and `FAIL AUTHENTICATE TOO_LONG` with standard-replies), a failure counted when an exchange is in flight, and the exchange ended. The line is charged as any other cut line. `parse_line_cmocka` covers it (upper and lower case, with tags, and a command that only starts the same way). Built and all CMocka suites pass; not re-run on a live server.

**Not changed:** bytes past the caps are not charged beyond the flat cost of a cut line (the review's item 2). It is small and bounded, about 11 KB/s of discarded input per client at most.

**Deploy:** the quit reasons `Excess Flood: tag region too large` and `Excess Flood: message region too large` are gone; a line over 64 KiB quits with `Excess Flood: line too long`. WebSocket clients no longer get Close 1009 for an overlong IRC line. Log alerts matching the old text need updating.

**With #285:** both change `authenticate_end()`'s callers in `m_authenticate.c`; #285 gives it a third argument, so the second to land passes 0 for it in `authenticate_too_long()`.
