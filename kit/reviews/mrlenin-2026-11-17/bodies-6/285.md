# #285 description update (second review, then simplified)

## Add at the end

```
**Changed after a second review, then simplified:** a client that pipelines AUTHENTICATE PLAIN and CAP END gets the challenge, then 906, and registers. Its credentials line then arrives after registration and could have started a new exchange with the base64 credentials as the mechanism name, sent on to IAuth or services. The first AUTHENTICATE of an exchange must now be an RFC 4422 mechanism name (upper case, at most 24 characters, for ECDSA-NIST256P-CHALLENGE); anything else gets 908 and 904 and reaches no backend. A client sending a lower-case mechanism such as `plain` now gets 904.
```
