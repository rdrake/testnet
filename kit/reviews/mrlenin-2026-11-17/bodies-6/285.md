# #285 description update (independent review, 2026-11-17 evening)

## Add at the end

```
**Changed after a second review:** A client that pipelines AUTHENTICATE PLAIN and CAP END is no longer sent the backend's challenge when the registration check is about to abort with 906 (same condition as `check_auth_finished()`); this covers services, IAuth and the asynchronous Keycloak challenges. Otherwise its credentials arrived after registration and, from a CAP 302 client, started a new exchange with the base64 credentials as the mechanism name, which went to IAuth or services. As a backstop, the first AUTHENTICATE of an exchange must be an RFC 4422 mechanism name (upper case, at most 24 characters, for ECDSA-NIST256P-CHALLENGE); anything else gets 908 and 904 and reaches no backend. A client sending a lower-case mechanism such as `plain` now gets 904.
```
